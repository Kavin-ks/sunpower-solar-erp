📋 Clear Stage-by-Stage Breakdown

Each stage enforces strict business gates before advancing to prevent financial losses and installation failures.

1

Customer Enquiry

Department: Sales & Customer Portal

Lead is captured from the website enquiry form or manually keyed in by sales. Captures contact details, address, desired capacity (1kW–5kW), and electricity bill.

Pre-Condition: Zero cost ($0). No payment is ever required at the enquiry stage.
customers   enquiries   enquiry_activities
2

Site Feasibility Survey

Department: Field Engineering / Technicians

A certified field technician visits the site. Measures shadow-free roof area (sq.ft), tilt angle, roof orientation, transformer capacity, and uploads photo evidence.

Strict Business Gate: A quotation CANNOT be created unless the site survey is officially APPROVED.
site_surveys   survey_photos   technicians
3

BOM & Quotation

Department: Sales & Pricing Engine

System builds an automated Bill of Materials (BOM) based on approved survey measurements. Pulls live inventory purchase costs to calculate exact gross profit margins.

Approval Gate: Discounts > 10% require Managing Director sign-off before customer dispatch.
solar_packages   package_boms   quotations
4

30% Advance Deposit

Department: Finance & Accounts

Customer accepts quotation. Quotation converts into a Sales Order with status PENDING_PAYMENT. Customer remits 30% advance via NEFT/UPI.

Finance Verification Gate: Finance verifies Bank UTR and records payment. Order moves to CONFIRMED only when Total Paid ≥ 30% Advance.
sales_orders   payments   invoices
5

Warehouse & Barcode Dispatch

Department: Warehouse & Logistics

Order confirmation automatically stages a Dispatch ticket and reserves stock. Warehouse picks equipment and scans individual serial number barcodes.

Traceability Gate: Dispatch cannot be marked DELIVERED without 100% verified barcode scans for high-value components.
inventory_stocks   dispatches   serial_numbers
6

Field Installation

Department: Field Installation Crew

Technician crew mounts panels, runs DC/AC cabling, verifies chemical earthing (< 5 Ohms), and synchronizes inverter with the DISCOM electrical grid.

Commissioning Sign-off: 5-point electrical safety checklist completed and customer digital signature captured on-site.
installations   installation_technicians   installation_photos
7

Warranty & Service Desk

Department: Customer Support & Service

Handover auto-generates 20-year solar panel and 2-year inverter warranties. Breakdown service tickets cross-reference hardware barcodes to waive replacement charges.

Warranty Automation: Defective parts under active warranty automatically billed at ₹0 to the customer.
warranties   service_tickets   service_parts
